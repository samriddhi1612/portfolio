Propeller Thrust Cage Design

UBC Rocket • Sep 2025 → Present • CAD · FEA · DFM · Prototyping

Overview

Designed and iterated a lightweight structural cage for UBC Rocket’s Thrust Vector Rocket (TVR) controlled-descent system[cite: 2]. The cage balances structural stiffness, low mass, propeller airflow, and manufacturability while directly interfacing with the motor and gimbal assemblies[cite: 2].

Key Requirements & Analysis

  • Structural Loads: Designed to withstand a worst-case 29.43 N axial thrust and 0.52 N·m torque under dynamic descent conditions[cite: 2].
  • FEA Optimization: Evaluated alternative strut geometries in ANSYS Static Structural to minimize mass while controlling maximum deflection and stress concentrations[cite: 2].
  • Vibration Mitigation: Minimized fastener count in high-vibration zones to prevent loosening and mechanical failure during flight[cite: 2].
  • Aerodynamics: Maximized open cross-sectional area to preserve clean airflow into and around the propellers[cite: 2].

Materials & Manufacturing

Selected Aluminum 6061 for primary load-bearing structural members for its strength-to-weight ratio and machinability, paired with 3D-printed PLA for lower-load interface connectors to enable rapid iteration and keep overall system mass down[cite: 2].

Tools & Skills

SolidWorks CAD, ANSYS Static Structural FEA, Design for Manufacturing (DFM), Material Selection, Rapid Prototyping, Static Thrust Rig Validation[cite: 2].
